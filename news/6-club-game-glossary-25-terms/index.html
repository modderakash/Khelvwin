6 Club Game Glossary — Quick Answer

Updated September 2026.

New players meet a lot of unfamiliar words on the 6 club game lobby: period, Wingo, big and small, wagering, KYC. This glossary sets out 25 of them in alphabetical order, each in one or two plain sentences, so you can read a screen or a notice without guessing.

Every term is defined as it is used in 6 Club Game, not as betting-forum slang. Where a term relates to money or safety, the entry says so. Some players write the name as 6club game; both spellings refer to the same platform, and this glossary uses the spaced form throughout.

Key takeaways

  • A handful of terms, namely round, period, timer, wallet and history, cover most of what the 6 Club Game screen shows.
  • Odds, house edge and RNG are descriptive terms; none of them can be used to predict or improve an outcome.
  • Bonus, wagering, gift code and referral are marketing terms; read the in-app terms for the actual conditions.
  • Self-exclusion and session limit are the two terms worth learning before the game terms.

How to Use This 6 Club Game Glossary

Scan the table for the word you have just seen, read the entry, then go back to the screen. Where a topic deserves a full article, the entry points to it, and the games hub lists every format mentioned here.

Two ideas appear in almost every 6 Club Game entry: round and chance. A round is a single timed draw; chance means the outcome is random and cannot be influenced by anything you know or do. Keep both in mind and most of the vocabulary falls into place.

Terms That Cause the Most Confusion

  • Odds and house edge are not the same thing. Odds describe a payout; house edge describes the long-run margin. Neither is something you can use.
  • Bonus and wagering travel together. A bonus is only as useful as the wagering condition attached to it, so read both figures in the app first.
  • Big and Small is about the digit, not the colour. A digit has both a size and a colour, and the two selections are settled separately.
  • Gift code and referral are both promotions, but one is a code you enter and the other is a link you share. Both are targets for impersonation scams.
  • History and RNG are opposites in purpose. One records the past; the other generates the future without reference to it.

That last point is the one worth memorising. Nothing on the 6 Club Game screen predicts anything, and no term in this glossary describes a way to improve a result.

Money Terms in 6 Club Game

Deposit, withdrawal, wallet, KYC and wagering are the terms that decide whether money moves the way you expect. The steps, methods and verification requirements are documented on the deposit and withdrawal page and in the withdrawal process guide, and this glossary does not repeat them.

One habit covers most money confusion: before you accept a 6 Club Game bonus or request a withdrawal, read the in-app notice for the current condition. Figures change; the notice is what applies.

Safety Terms Worth Learning First

OTP, self-exclusion and session limit are the three terms a new player should understand before the first round. OTP protects the login; the other two protect the budget. Both kinds of protection are set out on the responsible gaming page.

Scammers use 6 Club Game glossary words to sound official: they will mention your VIP level, offer a gift code, or ask for an OTP to verify a bonus. Knowing what each term actually means makes those messages easier to spot. The Google Safety Center is a good starting point for protecting a phone and its accounts.

Frequently Asked Questions

Straight answers about the words on the screen.

What is the most important term in the 6 Club Game glossary?

Round. Almost everything else describes a part of a round, a selection within one, or the money that moves after one. Once you understand that each round is an independent random draw, the rest of the vocabulary follows.

Does a higher VIP level change the odds?

No. VIP levels reflect account activity and may unlock stated perks, but every round is resolved by the same random process for every player. Odds never depend on level.

What is the difference between odds and house edge?

Odds describe what a winning selection pays relative to the stake. House edge describes the long-run margin the platform keeps across a very large number of rounds. Neither can be used to predict or improve a single result.

What does wagering mean on a bonus?

It is the amount of play required before bonus credit or its winnings can be withdrawn. The figure is stated in the app and can change, so read the current notice rather than relying on a number from elsewhere.

Which 6 Club Game terms relate to safety?

OTP, KYC, self-exclusion and session limit. OTP and KYC protect access and withdrawals; self-exclusion and session limit protect your budget. All four are worth understanding before you play.

Final Word

Twenty-five words are enough to read every screen in 6 Club Game with confidence. None of them hides a trick, because there is no trick: every round is chance, every term above is descriptive, and the two that matter most are the ones that set a limit. Learn those first, then the rest.
