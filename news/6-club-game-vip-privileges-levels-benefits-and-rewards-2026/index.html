What Is the VIP System in 6 Club Game?

The VIP system is a loyalty program. It rewards players based on:

  • Gameplay activity
  • Experience points (EXP)
  • Long-term participation

Higher VIP levels unlock more benefits.

Why VIP Privileges Matter

VIP privileges provide:

  • Extra rewards
  • Better rebate rates
  • Monthly bonuses
  • Deposit benefits

They are designed to give active users added value.

Overview of 6 Club Game VIP Levels

The VIP system has multiple levels, starting from VIP0 and moving upward.

Each level has:

  • A required EXP target
  • Specific benefits
  • Upgrade and maintenance rules

Users move up gradually, not instantly.

How VIP Experience Points (EXP) Work

EXP is earned through:

  • Valid gameplay
  • Betting activity
  • Platform participation

EXP is calculated automatically by the system.

EXP Calculation Rules

From the VIP rule description:

  • Valid betting converts to EXP
  • Conversion rate follows platform rules
  • EXP updates regularly

VIP data refreshes about every 10 minutes.

VIP Upgrade Standard Explained

To upgrade:

  1. Reach the required EXP
  2. Meet all upgrade conditions
  3. System promotes the account automatically

VIP statistics start counting from the VIP system launch time.

VIP Upgrade Order Rules

Important rule:

  • VIP levels can only increase one level per day
  • Jumping multiple levels at once is not allowed

This keeps progression fair and controlled.

VIP Level Maintenance Rules

Once a VIP level is reached:

  • Maintenance requirements must be met within 30 days
  • If completed, level remains active
  • If not, downgrade rules apply

Maintenance ensures continued activity.

VIP Downgrade Rules Explained

If maintenance fails:

  • EXP may be deducted
  • VIP level may drop
  • Benefits adjust to the lower level

Downgrades are automatic and system-based.

VIP Upgrade Bonus Explained

When a VIP level is reached:

  • Upgrade bonus can be claimed
  • Each level bonus can be claimed once only
  • Bonus is claimed from the VIP page

Unused upgrade bonuses do not repeat.

Monthly VIP Reward Explained

VIP members can:

  • Receive the highest monthly reward they qualify for
  • Claim only once per month
  • Unclaimed rewards reset after settlement

Monthly rewards are settled at 2:00 AM on the 1st of each month.

Monthly Reward Deduction Rule

If a user:

  • Claims a lower VIP reward
  • Then upgrades within the same month

The claimed reward is deducted when the higher reward is issued.

Real-Time Rebate Benefits

VIP rebates are calculated:

  • In real time
  • Across all games
  • Automatically

Higher VIP levels receive higher rebate rates.

Safe Deposit Benefits Explained

VIP members receive:

  • Extra benefits on safe deposits
  • Rewards based on VIP level

These benefits help reduce risk during deposits.

VIP Level Benefits Overview

Each VIP level may include:

  • Level-up rewards
  • Monthly rewards
  • Deposit rewards
  • Real-time rebates

Benefits increase gradually with level.

VIP1 Example Explained

From the image:

  • VIP1 requires a set EXP amount
  • Unlocks level-up reward
  • Includes monthly reward
  • Adds deposit reward percentage

This shows how benefits start at early levels.

Why VIP Progress Is Gradual

Gradual progress:

  • Prevents abuse
  • Rewards consistent play
  • Keeps system balanced

VIP is designed for long-term users.

How to Check VIP Status

Users can view:

  • Current VIP level
  • EXP progress
  • Payout time
  • Available rewards

All information is shown on the VIP page.

VIP Payout Time Explained

The VIP payout time shows:

  • Days remaining until settlement
  • Monthly reward timing

This helps users plan claims.

Common VIP Misunderstandings

Many users think:

  • VIP upgrades are instant
  • Rewards stack automatically

Both are incorrect.

VIP Rewards Do Not Accumulate

Important rule:

  • Monthly rewards do not accumulate
  • Unclaimed rewards reset

Always claim on time.

How VIP Rewards Affect Withdrawals

VIP rewards:

  • Follow wagering rules
  • Must meet conditions before withdrawal

They are not instant cash.

Best Practices for VIP Progress

  • Play consistently
  • Avoid rushing upgrades
  • Track maintenance requirements

Steady play is better than fast play.

VIP and Responsible Play

VIP benefits should:

  • Enhance experience
  • Not encourage overspending

Always set limits.

Can Support Change VIP Level?

Customer support:

  • Cannot modify VIP level
  • Cannot restore lost EXP

VIP rules are automated.

VIP Benefits and Bonus Interaction

VIP rewards may:

  • Add bonus balance
  • Require wagering
  • Follow expiry rules

Always check bonus details.

VIP and Bonus Reset Interaction

If a VIP bonus expires:

  • It resets like regular bonuses
  • VIP level remains unchanged

These systems are separate.

Why VIP Data Refreshes Regularly

Frequent refresh:

  • Keeps data accurate
  • Prevents delays
  • Shows real-time progress

This improves transparency.

VIP Levels and Fairness

VIP rules apply:

  • Equally to all users
  • Without manual adjustment

This ensures fairness.

How VIP Encourages Long-Term Play

VIP rewards:

  • Value consistency
  • Reward loyalty
  • Reduce short-term pressure

They are not designed for quick gains.

Tracking VIP History

VIP history helps users:

  • Understand progress
  • Review rewards
  • Plan next upgrades

Always review before making decisions.

When VIP Level Is Most Useful

VIP benefits matter most when:

  • Playing regularly
  • Managing budget well
  • Claiming rewards on time

Discipline improves VIP value.

Common Mistakes That Slow VIP Progress

Users often:

  • Skip days
  • Ignore maintenance rules
  • Miss monthly claims

Avoiding these improves progress.

VIP Privileges vs Regular Accounts

VIP users receive:

  • Extra rewards
  • Better rebate rates
  • Priority benefits

Regular users still have full access but fewer bonuses.

Why VIP Is Optional

VIP is optional:

  • No forced participation
  • No required spending

Users choose how active they want to be.

Final Thoughts on 6 Club Game VIP Privileges

The 6 Club Game VIP privileges system is clear, rule-based, and designed to reward steady participation. From upgrade bonuses and monthly rewards to real-time rebates and safe deposit benefits, VIP levels offer added value without hidden rules.
