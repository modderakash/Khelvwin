Managing 6club Game APK Notifications by Channel

Modern Android groups an app's notifications into channels, and each channel has its own switch, sound and importance level. This is the setting most players never find, and it is the one that solves the problem cleanly.

  1. Open Settings, then Apps, and find the 6 Club app.
  2. Tap Notifications. The top toggle controls everything; leave it on for now.
  3. Below it you will see the channels the app has registered, with names chosen by the developer. Switch off the ones you do not want.
  4. Tap any channel you keep to change its importance. Silent keeps the message in the shade without a sound or pop-up.
  5. Open Lock screen and set sensitive content to hidden, so an OTP or balance message is not readable by anyone glancing at the phone.

If the channel list is empty, the 6club game apk has not sent anything yet; open it once, then return. On MIUI the same screen is under Settings, Notifications and status bar, App notifications; One UI keeps it under Settings, Notifications, App notifications. Alternatively, long-press any notification as it arrives and tap the gear icon to jump straight to its channel.

Why Switching Off Promotional Alerts Helps Your Budget

There is nothing sinister about a bonus announcement. The problem is timing. A limit set in the morning is easy to keep until a push notification arrives late in the evening, with the phone already in your hand and nothing else planned. Each alert is a small decision point that you did not schedule, and decisions made on the spot are worse than decisions made in advance.

Muting the promotional channel removes those unscheduled decision points without removing your access to offers. The promotions page inside the app still shows everything current, and the terms are on the bonuses page, which you can read when you choose to. Pairing this with a deposit limit from the responsible gaming tools is the most effective budget control the 6club game apk can offer, and it costs nothing.

Do Not Disturb During Work Hours

Channel settings control what the app may send. Do Not Disturb controls when anything at all reaches you. Used together they cover both problems: the alert that should not exist, and the alert that should exist but not now.

  • Open Settings, Sound or Notifications, then Do Not Disturb, and create a schedule for your working hours and another for sleep.
  • Under Apps or Exceptions, allow only the apps that genuinely need to break through, such as calls from starred contacts. Leave the 6 Club app off that list.
  • Consider Focus mode or Digital Wellbeing, where you can pause the 6club game apk entirely for a set period; its icon goes grey on the home screen until the timer ends.
  • Check the schedule after any major system update, since some updates reset custom exceptions.

Result alerts that arrive while Do Not Disturb is active are not lost; they wait silently in the notification shade until the schedule ends. You lose nothing by delaying them, because the round outcome is recorded on the server regardless of whether you saw the notification at the moment it happened.

What a Notification Can and Cannot Tell You

Some forwarded messages claim that certain alerts, or the timing of them, indicate a favourable round ahead. They do not. Every game on the platform is chance-based and each round is independent. The 6club game apk sends a result alert after the outcome is decided, never before, and nothing in a promotional push relates to what the next round will produce. Treat any message that suggests otherwise as spam, whatever channel it arrives through.

One further safeguard: genuine notifications come only from the installed 6club game apk, from the platform's official SMS sender for OTPs, or from support in reply to a ticket you raised. A message on WhatsApp or Telegram asking you to install something from a link is not a notification, and installing from it bypasses the scan that Google Play Protect would normally offer. Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers on alerts, channels and quiet hours.

Can I turn off just the promotional notifications?

Yes. Android groups app notifications into channels with individual switches. Open Settings, Apps, the 6 Club app, Notifications, and switch off the promotional channel while leaving security and transaction alerts on.

Will I miss a result if Do Not Disturb is on?

No. The outcome is recorded on the server whether or not you saw the alert, and the notification waits silently in the shade until the schedule ends.

Why do OTP messages show on my lock screen?

Because lock-screen content is set to visible. In the app's notification settings, set sensitive content to hidden so codes and balance messages cannot be read by someone glancing at the phone.

Does a notification mean a good round is coming?

No. Every game is chance-based and result alerts are sent after an outcome is decided, never before. Any message suggesting otherwise is spam.

Where does the 6club game apk get its notification permission?

From Android, at first launch or the first time it tries to send one. You can revoke or restore it at any time under Settings, Apps, the 6 Club app, Notifications.
