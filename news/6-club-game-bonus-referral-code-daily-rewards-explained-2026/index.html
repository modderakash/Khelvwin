What Are Bonuses in 6 Club Game?

Bonuses are extra rewards given to users for:

  • Registering
  • Inviting friends
  • Logging in daily
  • Playing actively

Bonuses do not require special skills, but they follow clear rules.

Types of Bonuses Available

The platform offers several bonus categories:

  • Referral commission bonus
  • Attendance (daily login) bonus
  • Partner rewards
  • First-time deposit rewards

Each bonus has its own conditions.

Referral System Explained

The referral system allows users to earn rewards by inviting others.

How Referrals Work

  1. Get your personal invitation link
  2. Share it with friends
  3. Friends register using your link
  4. Friends make a deposit
  5. You earn commission

This system rewards active promoters.

How to Find Your Invitation Link

You can find your link in the Agency or Invitation section.

Options include:

  • Copy link
  • Share barcode
  • Share invitation poster

Only your personal link tracks referrals correctly.

Referral Commission Levels

The platform uses a tiered commission system.

Level 1 Commission

  • Minimum 0.6% per transaction
  • Applies to direct referrals

Level 2 Commission

  • 0.18% per transaction
  • Applies to second-level referrals

Commission is calculated automatically.

When Referral Commission Is Added

Referral earnings are:

  • Calculated daily
  • Added around 00:30 AM
  • Based on valid transactions

No manual claim is required.

How to Increase Referral Income

You can increase earnings by:

  • Inviting more users
  • Encouraging active play
  • Upgrading agent level

Higher activity leads to higher rewards.

Referral Bonus Conditions

To earn referral rewards:

  • Referral must register using your link
  • Referral must deposit real money
  • Fake or repeated accounts are not counted

Rule violations may cancel rewards.

Team Subordinates vs Direct Subordinates

The system tracks two types of referrals.

Direct Subordinates

  • Users who joined using your link

Team Subordinates

  • Users invited by your referrals

Both can generate commission.

Understanding the Agency Dashboard

The agency panel shows:

  • Yesterday’s total commission
  • Number of registered users
  • Deposit count
  • Deposit amount

This helps track performance.

Partner Rewards Explained

Partner rewards are special incentives for active agents.

They may include:

  • Extra bonus
  • Higher commission rate
  • Special promotions

Partner rewards depend on performance.

Attendance Bonus Explained

The attendance bonus rewards users for logging in every day.

How Attendance Works

  • Login daily
  • Rewards increase with consecutive days
  • Missing a day resets progress

Consistency is key.

Attendance Reward Amounts

Based on the image, rewards increase each day.

Example Attendance Rewards

  • Day 1: ₹6
  • Day 2: ₹16
  • Day 3: ₹36
  • Day 4: ₹66
  • Day 5: ₹166
  • Day 6: ₹666
  • Day 7: ₹1,666

Amounts may change based on updates.

How to Claim Attendance Bonus

To claim:

  1. Open Attendance section
  2. Login daily
  3. Bonus adds automatically

No extra steps needed.

Attendance Bonus Rules

  • One claim per day
  • Must login consecutively
  • Rewards reset if a day is missed

Follow rules to earn full value.

First Deposit Bonus

Some users receive a bonus on their first deposit.

Key Points

  • One-time reward
  • Minimum deposit required
  • Bonus terms apply

Always check bonus conditions.

Bonus Wagering Rules

Most bonuses require wagering.

What Wagering Means

  • You must place bets
  • Bonus amount must be used
  • Withdrawal allowed after wagering

Incomplete wagering blocks withdrawal.

Where to Check Bonus Status

You can check:

  • Bonus balance
  • Wagering progress
  • Bonus history

All details appear in the app.

Daily Reward Strategy for Users

To maximize rewards:

  • Login daily
  • Invite active users
  • Follow bonus rules
  • Track commission

Small daily actions add up.

Common Bonus Mistakes to Avoid

Avoid:

  • Using fake accounts
  • Sharing links publicly without care
  • Ignoring wagering rules
  • Missing attendance days

Mistakes may cancel bonuses.

How to Avoid Fake Bonus Scams

Fake messages may promise:

  • Instant bonus
  • Guaranteed income
  • Manual credit

Only trust bonuses shown inside the app.

Official Bonus Safety Tips

Remember:

  • No WhatsApp or Telegram support
  • No OTP requests
  • No manual bonus transfer

Safety protects rewards.

Bonus and Withdrawal Connection

Bonuses affect withdrawal.

Important:

  • Complete wagering
  • Follow limits
  • Check bonus balance

Understanding this avoids delays.

Bonus Expiry Rules

Some bonuses:

  • Have time limits
  • Expire if unused

Always read bonus rules.

Tax and Bonus Income

Referral and bonus income:

  • May be taxable
  • Depends on local rules

Users should keep records.

How to Track Referral Earnings

Use the agency dashboard to:

  • Monitor daily income
  • Check referral activity
  • Analyze performance

Transparency helps planning.

Is Referral Income Guaranteed?

No. Referral income depends on:

  • Referral activity
  • Deposits made
  • Valid accounts

No activity means no income.

Responsible Bonus Use

Bonuses should support fun play.

Tips:

  • Do not overplay
  • Avoid chasing losses
  • Use bonuses wisely

Responsible use improves experience.

Why Bonus Rules Exist

Rules help:

  • Prevent abuse
  • Ensure fairness
  • Protect system

Following rules keeps bonuses active.

Who Benefits Most from Bonuses?

Bonuses are ideal for:

  • New users
  • Regular players
  • Active referrers

Engagement increases rewards.

Bonus Transparency and Trust

The platform shows:

  • Clear bonus values
  • Visible rules
  • Trackable progress

This builds user trust.

Can Bonuses Be Withdrawn Directly?

No. Wagering is required.

Can I Combine Bonuses?

Usually no. Check rules.

Can Bonuses Expire?

Yes, if unused.

Final Thoughts on 6 Club Game Bonuses

The 6 Club Game bonus system offers multiple ways to earn extra value through referrals and daily activity. By understanding referral commission levels, attendance rewards, and wagering rules, users can make better decisions and avoid mistakes.

Bonuses are not guaranteed income but a reward for active and responsible participation. When used correctly, they add long-term value and improve the overall experience in 2026.
