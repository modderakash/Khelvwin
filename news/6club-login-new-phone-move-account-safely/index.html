6club Login on a New Phone — Quick Answer

Updated September 2026.

Your account is not stored on your handset. It lives on the platform's servers, tied to your registered mobile number and your password. Moving to a new phone is therefore a fresh 6club login on the new device: open the official site or app, enter the same number and password, complete any verification the screen asks for, and your balance, history and settings are all there waiting.

The moves that go wrong are rarely about the sign-in itself. They go wrong because the player forgot the password, let the registered SIM lapse, or left the old phone signed in and handed it to someone else. This guide deals with those three problems before they happen, and tells you what to do if one of them already has.

Key takeaways

  • Confirm your password and keep the registered number active before you touch the new phone.
  • A 6club login on the new handset needs only your registered number, your password and any code the screen requests.
  • Use the official site or the app from the official download page, never a forwarded link.
  • Log out of the old phone before you sell, gift or recycle it.
  • If your number changed, contact support through the official channel with your account details ready.
  • Every game on the platform is chance-based; a new phone changes nothing about outcomes.

Before You Switch: Three Things to Confirm

Most failed 6club login moves trace back to one of the checks below being skipped. Do them while the old phone still works, because each one is far easier with the old device in your hand.

  1. Know your password. If the old phone has been auto-filling it for months, you may not actually remember it. Log out and back in on the old device once to prove you can. If you cannot, use the reset option on the sign-in screen while your registered number still receives messages, and follow the on-screen notice for how the reset proceeds.
  2. Keep the registered number active. Verification codes for a 6club login go to the mobile number on your account. If you are changing SIM at the same time as changing phone, do the phone first and the SIM second, never both at once.
  3. Note your wallet state. Look at your balance and any pending withdrawal so you can confirm everything matches after the move. The wallet screens are explained on our deposit and withdrawal page.

A quick note on spelling, because it confuses people searching for help: players usually type 6club as one word, while we write 6 Club with a space, and both mean exactly the same platform.

6club Login Steps on the New Device

Start from the official homepage, or from the app obtained through our app download guide. Do not open a link someone sent you on WhatsApp or Telegram, however genuine it looks. Look-alike pages exist precisely to catch people who are setting up a new device and expect to type a password.

On the sign-in screen, select the correct country code, enter your registered number and your password. If the platform asks for a one-time code, it will arrive on the registered number; enter it and follow the on-screen notice if it expires. The field-by-field walkthrough is already in our login and registration guide, so we will not repeat it here.

Once the 6club login completes, do three quick checks. The balance should match what you noted. Your transaction history should be present. The profile details should be yours. If anything looks different, stop and contact support before you play a single round.

The pattern is simple. Anything about money or identity is on the server, and anything about convenience is on the handset. That is why the first sign-in on a new phone can feel slightly less smooth, with extra verification prompts, even though nothing about your account has changed.

Log Out of the Old Phone

This is the step people skip, and it is the one that matters most for safety. A phone with an open 6club login session is a phone that can be used by whoever holds it next, whether that is a buyer, a relative or a stranger who finds it.

  • Open the app or site on the old phone and use the log-out option in the account menu.
  • Remove the saved 6club login password from the old phone's browser or password manager.
  • If the old phone is being sold or given away, do a full factory reset after logging out.
  • If the old phone is lost, change your password from the new phone straight away and review your recent activity, which we cover in how to review your login history.

If Your Registered Number Has Changed

This is the awkward case. Verification codes go to a number you no longer control, so the normal 6club login route may not complete. Do not try to register a second account with the new number: duplicate accounts cause more problems than they solve and can lead to both being placed under review.

Instead, contact support through the official contact page on the site. Have ready the old number, the new number, your registered name and roughly when you last signed in. Support will tell you what proof they need; follow those instructions rather than anything a third party promises. If you still have the old SIM, keep it active until the change is confirmed.

6club Login Safety Habits for the Move

A new phone is a good moment to reset habits. General device-security guidance, such as screen locks, app permissions and recognising fake sign-in pages, is set out clearly at the Google Safety Center. For the platform specifically, keep these points in mind:

  • Set a screen lock on the new phone before you sign in anywhere.
  • Use a password you do not use on any other site.
  • Never share a one-time code with anyone, including someone claiming to be support.
  • Bookmark the official 6club login page so you never type the address into a search box under time pressure.
  • Set your money and time limits again if you had them, and check them on the responsible gaming page.

If you are new to the platform rather than just new to the phone, our 6 Club registration and login guide covers the account itself, from creating it to the first sign-in.

Frequently Asked Questions

Will I lose my balance when I sign in on a new phone?

No. Your balance, history and profile are stored on the platform servers, not on the handset. A 6club login on a new device shows the same account. The only things that do not transfer are conveniences such as saved passwords and remembered-device settings.

Can I stay signed in on both phones?

A session may remain open on the old phone until it expires or you log out. For safety, log out of the old device as soon as the new one works, and remove any saved password from it. Two open sessions is a risk, not a convenience.

What if I forgot my password before switching?

Use the reset option on the 6club login screen while your registered number can still receive messages, and follow the on-screen notice for how the reset works. Do this before you give up the old phone or SIM.

My registered number no longer works. What now?

Contact support through the official contact page with your old number, new number and registered name. Do not create a second account with the new number. Support will tell you what they need to update the record.

Do I need to verify identity again on a new phone?

Not usually, because verification status belongs to the account rather than the device. You may be asked for a one-time code or a device confirmation. Follow whatever the screen shows; it is a normal security step, not a sign of a problem.

Final Word

A new handset is the easy part. Prove your password works, keep the registered number alive, complete the 6club login on the new phone from the official source, then log out of the old one and reset it. Do those things in that order and the move is uneventful, which is exactly what you want from it.
