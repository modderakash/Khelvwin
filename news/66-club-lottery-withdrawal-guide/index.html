  • Your account has completed KYC verification
  • Any bonus wagering requirements are fulfilled
  • Your account is in good standing (not restricted or flagged)

These rules ensure fair use of platform bonuses and secure fund transfers.

How to Use the 66 Club Lottery Withdrawal Option (Step-by-Step)

Here’s a full walkthrough for withdrawing funds if you’re based in India:

1. Log in to Your Account

Go to the official 66 Club Lottery website. Log in using your registered email address or mobile number and your password.

2. Navigate to the “Withdraw” Section

Find this in your account dashboard, wallet section, or profile menu.

3. Choose a Withdrawal Method

In India, typical supported withdrawal methods may include:

  • UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, BHIM)
  • Paytm Wallet
  • Bank transfer (IMPS/NEFT)
  • E-wallets (some platforms support local options)

Use the same method as your deposit if possible—it speeds up processing.

4. Enter the Amount

Input the exact amount you wish to withdraw. Double-check that it meets the platform’s minimum threshold.

5. Confirm the Request

You may need to enter a one-time password (OTP) or verify through your linked email or mobile.

6. Wait for Processing

Funds typically arrive within minutes to 48 hours depending on your method and verification status.

Tips for a Smooth Withdrawal Experience

To ensure your 66 Club Lottery withdrawal goes through without issues:

  • Use correct payment info: Double-check UPI IDs, account numbers, or wallet handles
  • Withdraw using the same method as your deposit
  • Avoid multiple small withdrawals—combine into one to reduce fees
  • Keep a record: Save a screenshot or transaction ID
  • Clear any bonus conditions before withdrawing
  • Check limits: You may have daily or monthly caps based on account level

Are 66 Club Lottery Withdrawals Safe?

Withdrawals are generally safe if you use the official website and follow standard precautions:
