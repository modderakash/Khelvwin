1. UPI Payments (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM UPI)

UPI is one of the fastest and most popular payment options for online betting in India. It allows instant bank-to-bank transactions through various apps like Google Pay (GPay), PhonePe, and Paytm UPI.

How to Deposit Using UPI:

  1. Select UPI as your payment method.
  2. Enter your UPI ID or scan the QR code provided.
  3. Approve the payment request via Google Pay, PhonePe, or Paytm.
  4. Funds will be credited instantly to your 6 Club account.

Why Use UPI?

✅ Instant transactions with no extra charges.
✅ Highly secure and widely accepted.

2. Paytm Wallet & Paytm UPI

Paytm is one of the most widely used payment options in India. It offers two deposit methods on 6 Club: Paytm Wallet and Paytm UPI.

How to Deposit Using Paytm:

  1. Select Paytm Wallet or Paytm UPI in the deposit section.
  2. Enter your Paytm-linked phone number.
  3. Confirm the payment via OTP or Paytm PIN.
  4. The deposit will reflect instantly in your account.

Why Use Paytm?

✅ Quick transactions with minimal delays.
✅ Special cashback and promo offers.
✅ Secure and easy-to-use interface.

3. Google Pay (GPay)

Google Pay is a trusted UPI app that makes instant deposits quick and simple.

How to Deposit Using Google Pay:

  1. Choose GPay (Google Pay) as your deposit method.
  2. Enter the deposit amount.
  3. Approve the transaction in the GPay app.
  4. Money will be credited to your 6 Club account immediately.

Why Use Google Pay?

4. Net Banking (IMPS/NEFT/RTGS)

For larger deposits, Net Banking is a preferred option. It allows you to transfer funds from your bank account to 6 Club securely.

How to Deposit Using Net Banking:

  1. Choose Net Banking as your deposit method.
  2. Select your bank from the available options.
  3. Enter your bank login details and complete the transaction.
  4. Funds will be credited once the payment is processed.

Why Use Net Banking?

✅ Best for high-value transactions.
✅ Secure transactions with bank-level encryption.
✅ Works with all major Indian banks.

5. Bank Transfer (Direct Deposits)

For VIP and high-roller players, Bank Transfers offer a safe and reliable way to add funds to your 6 Club account.

How to Deposit Using Bank Transfer:

  1. Request 6 Club’s bank details from customer support.
  2. Transfer funds via Mobile Banking, IMPS, or NEFT.
  3. Share the transaction reference number with support for verification.
  4. The deposit will be added to your 6 Club account after confirmation.

Why Use Bank Transfers?

✅ Ideal for large deposits.
✅ Fully secure and verified.
✅ Direct and hassle-free transactions.
