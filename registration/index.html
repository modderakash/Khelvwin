Registration Guidelines

Here at 6 Club, we strive to maintain a safe, fair, and enjoyable experience for all users. Please read these rules carefully to ensure that your registration goes smoothly.

You must be at least 18 years old to create an account. This is to comply with our legal guidelines and ensure a responsible user base.

Each person is allowed to have only one account. This helps prevent abuse and ensures a level playing field for all participants.

Use Your Own Information:

When registering, make sure to provide your own personal details. Using false or outdated information, or someone else’s data, is strictly prohibited and could lead to the suspension or deletion of your account.

IP and Device Limits:

To avoid misuse, only one account per IP address or phone number is permitted. This prevents the creation of multiple accounts, which is not allowed.

We reserve the right to review and investigate any suspicious activities. Accounts found to be in violation of the rules may be suspended or permanently banned.

Cheating is not allowed under any circumstances. Violators will have their accounts permanently closed to maintain fairness for all users.

If you need to create a new account for special reasons, please contact our support team to request authorization.

Common Registration Issues and How to Solve Them

We understand that sometimes things don’t go as planned. Here are some common problems you might face during registration and how to resolve them:

Forgot Your Password?

  • Click the “Forgot Password” link on the login page. You’ll receive an email or SMS with instructions on how to reset your password.

Didn’t Receive a Verification Code?

  • If the verification code doesn’t arrive, wait a few minutes and try again. Make sure your phone number is entered correctly. If the issue persists, contact customer support.

Unable to Create an Account?

  • Check if you already have an account. Remember, only one account per person is allowed. If you believe you need another account for any valid reason, reach out to our support team for approval.

Trouble Logging In?

  • If you can’t log in after registration, make sure you’re using the correct phone number and password. If the problem continues, contact our support team for assistance.
